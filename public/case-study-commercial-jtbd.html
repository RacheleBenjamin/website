What are Jobs to Be Done?

JTBD go beyond activities to capture the underlying need.

The framework yields underlying needs, gaps, opportunities, and other “hires” (tools to accomplish the job) — helping us build out-of-the-box solutions.

Motivation → Job → Activity → Hire. Example: To make a difference → Stay informed about changes → Reading news → Browser.

Jobs to Be Done · Motivations

Users' motivations were variable

Part of the JTBD framework is understanding what users really want out of their browser jobs. We captured five major motivations:

⚖️
Balance
🚀
Get Ahead
🌱
Personal Growth
💛
Make a Difference
🛡️
Stability
📢 Users inform for stability, to get ahead, or to make a difference

The same job can be driven by completely different motivations depending on the user's context and career stage.

🧪 Users research for personal growth, mental stimulation, or stability

Research isn't just a task — it's a vehicle for multiple personal and professional goals.

🗣️ Users influence to make a difference, get ahead, or for personal growth

Influence is fundamentally about impact — but users define impact in different ways.

Insight
Features built around tasks alone miss the deeper motivations driving engagement. AI features should adapt to the why, not just the what.

Key Finding

When users engage with Gen AI at work, here's what's missing

💡
Remember / Have Templates Handy
Users start from scratch each time to produce specialized AI output — letters, spreadsheets, graphs, process maps.
💡
Internal & External Context
Gen AI must reference internal documentation so it can interpret questions appropriately and provide relevant answers.
💡
Detect Tone
Users probe Gen AI to give the perfect tailored response — tone is vital in professional written communication.
In their words
“Being able to know that I'm presenting myself appropriately in this written format, where tone is so vital, is key to me.” — Tejal, Consulting, Business Analyst
“AI has handcrafted a template [for Medicare notes]. It'll purposely leave a spot where you put certain information. I just delete what's not applicable. It's wonderful.” — Brianna, Healthcare, Nurse
“We have unique identifiers within our system for accounts, contacts, whatever it may be, and I don't believe that Gen AI would be able to understand... It's a very specific situation.” — Marc-Louis, Event Services, Product Analyst
12
In Summary
Finding

10 core Jobs to Be Done define the commercial browser user. Top AI opportunities cluster around Research, Create, Collaborate, Get Answers, Interpret, and Influence.

Insight

Enterprise users demand explicit data governance and compliance transparency before adopting AI features. Security and auditability are table stakes, not differentiators.

Recommendation

Design for explicit data governance and compliance transparency. Give users full control over communication while automation handles background tasks. Build a New Tab Page that connects users to what motivates them at work.

Impact

Insights shaped the vision and strategy for the Enterprise Edge AI-first browser, informing the roadmap for 10M+ global users. Browser for Work scenarios were showcased at Ignite 2026.

Defining what an AI-first browser for work should be.

Across 16 participants spanning five role categories — from IT and HR to customer-facing and research — two threads held. Users want AI that partners, not replaces. And the browser for work must understand not just what people do, but why they do it.
