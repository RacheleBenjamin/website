Adoption is earned, not assumed

Institutions sit on a spectrum — from AI-mandated to AI-blocked

🟢 Low friction

Leadership encourages — even incentivizes — AI as a competitive necessity.

"Leadership were like, how can we be a key competitor in AI if we're not even using it?"— Krystina R, Financial Services

🟡 Medium friction

AI is encouraged, but it's hard to see where it helps a specific team.

"My company loves AI… but when it comes to my team, I don't see as much benefit."— Kendal E, Insurance

⛔ High friction

Outside bodies — agencies, clients, funders — block exposing data to AI entirely.

"Just not in our finance team right now… we don't know enough with sensitive information for people to feel comfortable."— Kelly F, Nonprofit Finance

And two individual barriers

Job anxiety

"I'd lose my creative outlet… our jobs would become more like training AI rather than doing the work."— Kendal E, Entry/Mid IC, Insurance

Opportunity: agentic systems automate the boring parts and leave the craft intact.

Stakes awareness

"I'm talking about company numbers that impact their decision making… I need to make sure that data is right. Absolutely right."— Jerome T, People Manager, Construction

Reporting: prompt human review whenever numbers are involved.

When AI relies on bad data, the stakes escalate

Warm

It's slow — users have to backtrack and redo.

"If you've gone wrong, it takes much of your time… it takes hours."— Humera G, IT System Analyst, Technology

Hot

It undermines their reputation with stakeholders.

"It's a level of trust from a partner giving you financial information… knowing that data was exposed — that's it."— Kelly F, Accounts Receivable, Nonprofit Finance

Boiling

It impacts the business, direct reports, or triggers a compliance failure.

"We're federally funded and have to report this information responsibly."— Kelly F, Accounts Receivable, Nonprofit Finance

Mitigation

A human in the loop at two moments: after extracting data or manipulating numbers, and before submitting work or nudging another person.

Recommendation: Users need to move between apps. There's no 'winning in the browser' without winning everywhere.

Deliverables: Interactive sheet, Scrolly Story

Design impact: Designs informed by the co-design component: Lighter-weight confirmations and a 'walk me through it' button.

Strategic impact: Unblocked the next step of admin 1:1s to solve for institutional frictions.
