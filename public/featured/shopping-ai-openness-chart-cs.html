Activity Differences in AI Openness: Within-Subjects Mixed Models

Model 1 — Open to any AI help: glmer(any_help ~ Activity + (1 | ResponseId), family = binomial), where any_help = 1 if the respondent picked ideas, part, or all (vs no help).

Model 2 — Wants AI to do the work, among helpers: glmer(ai_does ~ Activity + (1 | ResponseId), family = binomial), restricted to respondents who would accept any AI help; ai_does = 1 if they picked "complete part" or "complete all" (vs "ideas only").

Estimated marginal means (emmeans) with Holm-adjusted p-values across the 136 pairwise contrasts per model; only contrasts with adjusted p < .05 are shown in the hover tips.
