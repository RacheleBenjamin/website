Plan

Study setup to help the team decide

Remote unmoderated concept testing of discoverability, impressions, and usefulness. Two user groups; mega-menu tested after Tab Centre.

Our questions mapped onto two design principles:

A tool for everyone

  • When and why do different user groups need a tab tool?
  • How well or poorly do tab manager and mega-menu help users manage tab clutter?

Bring similar concepts together

  • Will users have easier access to features like Recently closed tabs and Workspaces from these entry points?

Two user groups in remote unmoderated sessions. Both have 10+ tabs open at a time and hadn't used vertical tabs in the past month.
